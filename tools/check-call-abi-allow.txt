# tool_startup.S loads the two flags into d0/d1 AND pushes them, so the call
# is right under either convention; the toolchain file says why it is the one
# unpinned call from assembly (cmake/toolchain-m68k-amigaos.cmake).
asm-call src/tools/tool_startup.S ami_rt_cpu_select both conventions loaded at the call site
